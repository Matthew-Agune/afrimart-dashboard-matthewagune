# afrimart-dashboard-matthewagune
Interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing AfriMart sales performance, revenue, profit, and product trends.


##Project Overview
The AfriMart KollyBright Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, and product performance across different countries and time periods.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that can support business decision-making, performance monitoring, and identification of sales trends.
---

## Business Questions Answered

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:
- What is the total revenue generated by AfriMart?
- What is the total profit generated from sales?
- How many units have been sold?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- Which country generates the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most to total profit?
- Which products have the highest number of units sold?
- How does sales performance change from month to month?
- What is the revenue trend over time?
- What are the top 5 performing products?
- How does revenue vary across products and countries?
- Wow does business performance change across different years?

---

## Key KPIs

The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:
- Total Revenue ₦253,333,500 
- Total Profit  ₦58,966,950 
- Total Units Sold 28,502 
- Profit Margin 23.28%

##Tools used
Microsoft Excel – Data analysis, calculations, PivotTables, PivotCharts, dashboard development and visualization.
Excel PivotTables – Used to summarize and analyze sales data.
Excel PivotCharts – Used to create interactive visualizations.
Excel Slicers/Filters – Used to enable interactive filtering by country and year.
GitHub – Used for project documentation, version control, and portfolio presentation
